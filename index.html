Text Highlighter

A Chrome extension for focused reading with voice-activated follow-along, supporting English and Ge'ez script (Tigrinya)

Home Privacy Policy

What it does

Text Highlighter places a visible highlight box around the current word or line and lets you step through text at your own pace. It also includes a voice follow mode that listens to you read aloud and automatically advances the highlight to follow along.

Voice Follow New

Read aloud and the highlight follows your voice in real time. Works with English and Tigrinya (via Amharic speech recognition).

Smooth Highlighting

The highlight glides between positions with adjustable animation speed. Supports outline, underline, and fill styles.

Multi-line Mode

Highlight 1 to 8 lines at once. In voice mode, the highlight slides by one line at a time so your reading position stays centered.

Resume Reading

Remembers where you left off. Refresh or close the page — the highlight resumes from your last position within 24 hours.

Supported sites

  • Google Slides — edit and presentation mode. Text boxes read individually.
  • AbbaSelama.org — prayer texts with content change detection, psalm jump by number.
  • GeezExperience.com — Tigrinya/English Bible with two-column support, verse jump by number.
  • Any website — toggle ON from the popup or press Option+H.

Voice follow mode

Press V to start voice follow. A green mic indicator appears in the top-right corner. Read aloud and the highlight advances automatically as it recognizes your speech.

  • On Ge'ez sites (AbbaSelama, GeezExperience), uses Amharic speech recognition — the closest supported language to Tigrinya.
  • Press T to toggle between Amharic and English recognition without stopping voice follow.
  • Ge'ez script matching uses phonetic normalization, consonant family weighting, and prefix matching to handle cross-language differences.
  • If the algorithm loses track, it automatically re-syncs or advances after a brief delay.

Popup settings

Click the extension icon in the toolbar to customize:

  • On/Off — activate or deactivate on the current page
  • Color — 5 presets or a custom color picker
  • Style — Outline, Underline, or Fill
  • Words — Line mode (default), 1w, 5w, or 10w
  • Lines — 1 to 8 lines per highlight (default: 3)
  • Voice Follow — toggle voice mode on/off
  • Liturgy (Qidasie) — Off, Tigrinya, or English column in Google Slides
  • Box speed — highlight animation speed (default: 1650ms)
  • Scroll speed — auto-scroll speed (default: 6400ms)
  • Border thickness — highlight border width

Verse & psalm jump

On GeezExperience and AbbaSelama, type number keys to jump directly:

  • GeezExperience — verse number (e.g. 15 for verse 15). Jumps within the column you're reading.
  • AbbaSelama — psalm/section number from headings.

How to install

  1. Install from the Chrome Web Store
  2. Go to any webpage
  3. Press Option+H or click the extension icon and toggle ON
  4. Use . to advance, , to go back, or press V for voice follow
