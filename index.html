What it does

Text Highlighter places a visible box around the current word or line and lets you step through text at your own pace. It includes voice follow mode that listens to you read aloud and advances the highlight automatically, a teleprompter for silent reading, Tiglish transliteration for Ge'ez script, and a training data recorder for building Tigrinya speech-to-text models.

Voice Follow

Read aloud and the highlight follows your voice in real time. Works with English and Tigrinya (via Amharic speech recognition).

Teleprompter New

Auto-scrolls text for silent reading. Adjust speed with arrow keys. Pauses and resumes with spacebar.

Tiglish Transliteration New

Shows Latin pronunciation below Ge'ez text. Helps readers who can speak Tigrinya but can't read the script.

Training Data Collection New

Record yourself reading highlighted text to generate labeled audio data for Tigrinya speech-to-text training.

Smooth Highlighting

The highlight glides between positions with adjustable animation speed. Supports outline, underline, and fill styles.

Multi-line Mode

Highlight 1 to 8 lines at once. In voice mode, the highlight slides by one line at a time.

Resume Reading

Remembers where you left off. Refresh or close the page — the highlight resumes from your last position within 24 hours.

Verse & Psalm Jump

Type a number to jump directly to a verse or psalm on supported Bible sites.

Supported sites

  • Google Slides — edit and presentation mode. Text boxes read individually.
  • AbbaSelama.org — prayer texts with content change detection, psalm jump by number.
  • GeezExperience.com — Tigrinya/English Bible with two-column support, verse jump by number.
  • BibleGateway.com — chapter navigation and verse highlighting.
  • Any website — toggle ON from the popup or press Option+H.

Voice follow mode

Press V to start voice follow. A green mic indicator appears in the top-right corner. Read aloud and the highlight advances automatically as it recognizes your speech.

  • On Ge'ez sites (AbbaSelama, GeezExperience), uses Amharic speech recognition — the closest supported language to Tigrinya.
  • Press T to toggle between Amharic and English recognition without stopping voice follow.
  • Ge'ez script matching uses phonetic normalization, consonant family weighting, and prefix matching to handle cross-language differences.
  • If the algorithm loses track, it automatically re-syncs or advances after a brief delay.

Teleprompter mode

Press P to start the teleprompter. The page scrolls at a steady pace so you can read without touching the keyboard. Use ↑ / ↓ to adjust speed, Space to pause and resume.

Tiglish transliteration

Press G to show Latin pronunciation below each Ge'ez word (e.g. ሰላም → selam). Useful for readers who can speak Tigrinya but are still learning to read the Ge'ez script. Toggle off by pressing G again.

Training data collection

Built-in recording workflow for generating labeled Tigrinya speech data:

  1. Press D to start recording — read the highlighted text aloud.
  2. Words are underlined as matched. Gaps fill automatically (if word 1 and 3 match, word 2 gets underlined too).
  3. Recording auto-stops when the last word matches and 70% coverage is reached.
  4. Auto-advances to the next box. Press D again to record the next chunk.
  5. Press Escape to discard a bad take.
  6. Press Shift+D to export all recordings as chunk_XXXX.wav files + metadata.csv.

The transcript is the known page text (not speech engine output), so labels are 100% accurate. Output format is ready for Whisper fine-tuning.

Popup settings

Click the extension icon in the toolbar to customize:

  • On/Off — activate or deactivate on the current page
  • Color — 5 presets or a custom color picker
  • Style — Outline, Underline, or Fill
  • Words — Line mode (default), 1w, 5w, or 10w
  • Lines — 1 to 8 lines per highlight (default: 3)
  • Voice Follow — toggle voice mode on/off
  • Liturgy (Qidasie) — Off, Tigrinya, or English column in Google Slides
  • Box speed — highlight animation speed
  • Scroll speed — auto-scroll speed
  • Border thickness — highlight border width

How to install

  1. Install from the Chrome Web Store
  2. Go to any webpage
  3. Press Option+H or click the extension icon and toggle ON
  4. Use . to advance, , to go back, or press V for voice follow
  5. Press ? to see all keyboard shortcuts
