What it does

Text Highlighter places a visible box around the current word or line and lets you step through text at your own pace. It includes voice follow mode that listens to you read aloud and advances the highlight automatically, a teleprompter for silent reading, and Tiglish transliteration for Ge'ez script.

Voice Follow

Read aloud and the highlight follows your voice in real time. Works with English and Tigrinya. Includes voice commands for hands-free control.

Teleprompter

Auto-scrolls text for silent reading. Adjust speed with arrow keys. Pause and resume with spacebar.

Tiglish Transliteration

Shows Latin pronunciation below Ge'ez text (e.g. ሰላም → selam). Helps readers learning the script.

Smooth Highlighting

The highlight glides between positions with adjustable animation speed. Supports outline, underline, and fill styles.

Multi-line Mode

Highlight 1 to 8 lines at once. In voice mode, the highlight slides by one line at a time.

Resume Reading

Remembers where you left off. Refresh or close the page — the highlight resumes from your last position.

Verse & Psalm Jump

Type a number to jump directly to a verse or psalm on supported Bible sites.

Works Anywhere

Built-in support for Google Slides, AbbaSelama, GeezExperience, and BibleGateway. Activate on any other site too.

Supported sites

  • Google Slides — edit and presentation mode. Text boxes read individually.
  • AbbaSelama.org — prayer texts with content change detection, psalm jump by number.
  • GeezExperience.com — Tigrinya/English Bible with two-column support, verse jump by number.
  • BibleGateway.com — chapter navigation and verse jump by number or voice.
  • Any website — toggle ON from the popup or press Option+H (Mac) / Alt+H (Windows).

Voice follow mode

Press V to start voice follow. A green mic indicator appears in the top-right corner showing ትግ (Tigrinya) or EN (English). Read aloud and the highlight advances automatically.

  • On Ge'ez sites (AbbaSelama, GeezExperience), uses Amharic speech recognition — the closest Chrome-supported language to Tigrinya.
  • Press T to toggle between Tigrinya and English recognition without stopping.
  • If the algorithm loses track, it automatically re-syncs or advances after a brief delay.

Teleprompter mode

Press P to start the teleprompter. The page scrolls at a steady pace for silent reading. Use ↑ / ↓ to adjust speed, Space to pause and resume.

Tiglish transliteration

Press G to show Latin pronunciation below each Ge'ez word (e.g. ሰላም → selam). Useful for readers who can speak Tigrinya but are still learning to read the Ge'ez script. Press G again to hide.

Popup settings

Click the extension icon in the toolbar to customize:

  • On/Off — activate or deactivate on the current page
  • Color — 5 presets or a custom color picker
  • Style — Outline, Underline, or Fill
  • Words — Line mode (default), 1w, 5w, or 10w
  • Lines — 1 to 8 lines per highlight (default: 3)
  • Voice Follow — toggle voice mode on/off
  • Liturgy (Qidasie) — Off, Tigrinya, or English column in Google Slides
  • Box speed — highlight animation speed
  • Scroll speed — auto-scroll speed
  • Border thickness — highlight border width

How to install

  1. Install from the Chrome Web Store
  2. Go to any webpage
  3. Press Option+H (Mac) / Alt+H (Windows) or click the extension icon and toggle ON
  4. Use . to advance, , to go back, or press V for voice follow
  5. Press Shift+? to see all keyboard shortcuts
